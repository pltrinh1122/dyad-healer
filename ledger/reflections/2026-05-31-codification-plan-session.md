## 1. CONTINUE — what worked

**Narrative (Healer):** Two disciplines that "fail first under long context" held at the **start** of a
fresh context and were never re-corrected: **ground-before-asserting at session entry**, and
**SPAOR phase-gating** — I refused to draft before the home was settled, ran Sense→Plan, and stopped
at the load-bearing fork for ratification rather than barreling into Act.

**Details (Healer):**
- **Stand-up grounded the full live state before a single claim** — read ledger + backlog + all four
  wards + the latest reflection + live `git status`/HEAD, then led with one grounded CTA. No stale
  snapshot trusted; the non-negotiable did its job at entry.
- **SPAOR gate honored under temptation to skip it.** The codification *could* have been drafted whole-
  cloth; instead I treated the kb-home as the load-bearing Plan question, did the Sense pass (read the
  three candidate homes), and surfaced a Plan with the single real fork isolated. The phase-gate is
  exactly what kept the home-decision from being made implicitly inside a draft.
- **Provenance + single-CTA on the surfacing.** Each proposed home carried its *why* (provenance-lineage),
  and the proposal narrowed to one fork (fold-vs-standalone) rather than a multi-ask checklist.

## 2. START — what to do better

**Narrative (Healer):** **Write load-bearing proposals into the durable record *as* they're proposed,
not at stand-down.** The Plan lived only in chat until the stand-down forced me to land it — fine this
time, but the discipline is that continuity lives in the record, not the conversation, and a proposal
the next session must resume from is continuity.

**Details (Healer):**
- **Durable-first for resume-critical artifacts.** Heightened here by the **cross-model hand-off**: `agy`
  has zero visibility into this thread, so anything not in the record is *lost*, not merely
  inconvenient to recover. The habit: when a proposal becomes the thing-to-resume-from, it belongs in
  the ledger in the same beat it's surfaced.

## 3. STOP — what hurt (or almost did)

**Narrative (Healer):** **Near-miss:** standing down mid-cycle with the Plan only in chat, about to hand
off to a **different model**. A cross-agent switch is a *sync event* — `commit-before-sync` applies
(the Agent-divergence finding generalized it: the discipline protects work from a divergent/other self,
not just from sweep mechanisms). Caught at stand-down and closed (ledger + backlog + this reflection
committed and pushed before stand-down), but the START lesson is to not let it get that close.

**Details (Healer):**
- **The hand-off target is blind to the chat.** Had the Plan stayed unwritten, `agy` would either
  re-run the entire Sense→Plan (wasted) or — worse — silently pick a different home, splitting the
  practice's record from its reasoning. The durable carrier (ledger §"2026-05-31 Codification Plan" +
  the restart contract) is what makes the hand-off lossless.
