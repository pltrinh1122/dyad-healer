When in **EMS mode (Exception posture — a patient is seized / under care)**, do not get distracted by
other interruptions — e.g. deep-reading and writing up cairn's mechanism review, or composing elaborate
side-responses — while the active case runs. (Operator correction, 2026-06-06, during the wu-wei #1793
case.)

**Why:** Exception posture is urgency + **minimum-cut** + bounded authority. Minimum-cut governs my
*attention*, not just the size of the intervention. Adjacent work dilutes focus on the cut and spends
attention/tokens that belong to the patient. Note: even an Operator-handed side-task ("review cairn,
adopt as you see fit") is not a license to rabbit-hole mid-EMS.

**How to apply:** triage every interruption by *"does this directly serve the active cut?"*
- Serves the cut → do it (e.g. arming the dm-watch monitor to detect the patient's reply WAS in-scope).
- Doesn't → acknowledge briefly, park it for post-discharge, don't deep-dive.
Keep responses tight in EMS. The full review/comparison/adoption happens at discharge or in the retro,
not during the cut. Discharge is the gate that reopens normal Authoring-pace breadth.
