Our craft is dispensing **self-healing methods and guards** (capability-builders) — **not bug-fixing** for the patient. Bug-fixing *for* the patient is never our move: it does the patient's work, breeds dependence (anti-Telos), and violates point-don't-fix.

**Why:** the Telos is the patient's self-healing efficacy + our obsolescence (Summit 1). A method/guard raises self-heal capacity → less need for us. A bug-fix just patches one defect and creates dependence. Surfaced 2026-06-07 (Operator rubs) when a drafted rx (the case-05 discard-invariant guard) was caught as a *bug-fix* dressed as a guard — dropped.

**How to apply — posture-keyed:**
- **Not seized (Prevention/maturation):** dispense self-healing methods/guards — or nothing.
- **Seized (Exception/rescue):** point at the **minimum-cut** for the patient to ratify — still not a fix-for-them.
- **Bug-fixing-for-the-patient:** never; neither posture licenses it.

Method-rx still clears [[feedback_uncertainty-is-law]] + [[feedback_deliver-prescription-post-research]] per instance (certain blind spot, not arguable; deliver vs post). Watch the inverse trap: don't project our *own* maturation frontier (e.g. prose→guard, [[feedback_bind-marker]]) onto the patient as its prescription. The real case-05 self-healing gap was **confabulation** (false self-diagnosis), a method target — not the discard bug.
