The Operator provides their reflection-POV (the dyadic Step-2 half) **only on CONTINUE**. START/STOP are *corrections*, and the Operator delivers + the dyad integrates them **in realtime via [FEEDBACK]** during the session — so by reflection-time they are already settled; a retrospective Operator-POV on them would re-litigate a cycle the grain has already moved past. CONTINUE (affirmation of what worked) is the half the Operator does *not* deliver in realtime, so the reflection is its natural home — the Operator's CONTINUE-POV carries information the Healer lacks, whereas a START/STOP-POV would only echo the realtime FEEDBACK.

**Why:** wu-wei + scientific method — realtime FEEDBACK is the experiment's corrective signal applied immediately; reflection-CONTINUE records the confirmed positive results. Don't duplicate at reflection-time what was already corrected at realtime.

**How to apply:** the Healer still authors all of CONTINUE/START/STOP at Step-1 draft; but expect/solicit Operator-POV only on CONTINUE, and treat the session's in-flight [FEEDBACK] as the START/STOP Operator-POV already delivered. kb-codification candidate for the reflection-discipline (Pillar-5; Operator ratifies).
