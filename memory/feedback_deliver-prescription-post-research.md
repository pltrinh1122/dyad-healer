Two Healer outputs carry different authority and so take different channel-verbs:
- **Prescription** = post-incidence (an observed wound, grounded) → **action-authority** → **deliver** it (active push, expects triage, via the Requirement Intake channel).
- **Research** = pre-incidence (a theory predicting future incidences, held falsifiably) → **prioritization / attention authority only** → **post** it (make it available on the record; the patient pulls it; no demand to act).

Research *feeds and sharpens* prescription; it never substitutes for it or borrows its action-authority. A cross-case finding is a hybrid: high-authority retrodiction of the cases it explains + lower-authority forecast of the cases it predicts.

**Authority is dynamic, earned empirically (scientific method).** Research authority is not fixed-low — a theory (stated as a clear falsifiable invariant) **accrues authority as empirical evidence substantiates it** (retrodiction + confirmed forward predictions). Empirical confirmation is the **conversion point**: it turns *posted* research into authority that can back a *delivered* prescription, and yields faster diagnoses. So post-vs-deliver is a snapshot of current grounding, not a permanent caste.

**Why:** wu-wei — the force of the channel-act must match the grounding of the claim. Over-*delivering* research (pushing a prediction with action-weight) is force-against-nature and risks iatrogenic over-care. Authority tracks grounding-depth: a prediction has one more inferential hop to be wrong than an observed wound, until incidents close that gap.

**How to apply:** before pushing any patient-facing flag, ask "is this an observed wound (deliver) or a prediction (post)?" Post research to our durable record; reserve delivery for grounded wounds. Pairs with [[uncertainty-is-law]]. Pending formal kb codification (Pillar-5 queue).
