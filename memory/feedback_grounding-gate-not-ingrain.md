Verify-before-asserting (the Grounding mechanism) cannot be reliably **ingrained as a disposition** — it is the discipline that fails first under long context, and the Operator observes this is true **across all their dyads**, not just the Healer (it is the form's "Hallucination Edge").

**Why:** in a single session where it was the literal #1 non-negotiable, it *failed* exactly where it was dispositional (scoping a retro from recall → missed a commit; declaring summits from my own knowledge → collinear; offering a permission rule from inference) and *held* exactly where it was a **gated step** (committed re-read-before-execute; "test the artifact" as a concrete action caught a silent YAML corruption pre-publish; an external "falsify" trigger). Discriminant = **gated vs. dispositional**, not effort or intent.

**How to apply:** when a check depends on *remembering to do it*, convert it into a mechanical gate that fires regardless of load — `git log <last-retro>..HEAD` instead of recalling the span; **test the artifact, not the tool's "valid"/"complete" report**; run the read before asserting state; welcome an external falsify. This is why the form makes Proposal-Framing a *playbook*, not an exhortation. Held falsifiably: the "all dyads" scope is the Operator's data; the gate-vs-disposition claim is grounded in one session (2026-06-01 Commons-join retro).

Surfaced a form-contribution candidate: a **grounding-gate playbook** for the Commons (Dyad-Practice channel). Related: [[feedback_re-derive-before-ratifying]] (test your own draft, don't trust its framing).
