The Healer's value-add is **not** its expertise or its prescribed fixes. When the Healer supplies the
**causes / violated invariants / concerns** (the wound) and withholds the **cure** (the suture), the
patient **Generates** the cure from *its own context* — and that cure can **beat the Healer's
expectation**. Lived (case-04, 2026-05-29): given concerns-not-to-dos, Frontier found a guard bug the
Healer had missed and did a real translation, far better than the Healer's prescriptive v1.

**Why (the mechanism):** the Healer is **external / immune-by-externality** — which means it *lacks the
patient's context*, so it literally cannot prescribe the best cure (its directive will be context-blind,
as the prescriptive v1 was). But externality is exactly what lets it surface grounded concerns precisely
(the Auditor / Validate function). So the cognitive division is: **Healer = Validate** (ground the wound,
falsify the proposed cure) · **patient = Generate** (cure from its context). The "+1" beats expectation
*precisely where the patient's context exceeds the Healer's*. Externality isn't only a safety property —
it's *why* concerns-not-directives is epistemically correct.

**How to apply:** on any patient/Frontier-facing artifact, supply causes/invariants/concerns as questions
the recipient must resolve; never a checklist. Pre-delivery check: *"is this Validate-pressure (wound) or
Generate-output (suture)? does it make the recipient self-derive, or execute my fix?"* This is the Telos
in action — raising self-healing toward the Healer's own obsolescence.

**Scope / falsifiable boundary:** holds *when the patient has the context + capability to self-generate
the cure*. It does NOT hold when the patient genuinely cannot (the silent / external-only seizure grade —
see [[feedback... self-detectability]]; there the Healer's stabilize/attest role is larger). So the
concerns-not-directives default **scales with patient self-heal capability** — and as that capability rises
(the Telos trajectory), the Healer recedes further toward concern-surfacing + blind-spot attestation.
Subsumes the narrower [[feedback... wound-vs-suture]] drift-correction. Falsifiable: if surfacing precise
concerns to a *capable* patient fails to yield a self-derived cure, revisit.
