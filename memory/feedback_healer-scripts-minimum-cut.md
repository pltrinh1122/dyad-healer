The Healer authors orient (S8 Healer→Patient seed) and closing handoffs (S8
Healer→Patient discharge) for the Frontier Operator to deliver. Two patterns
about HOW to author them:

## (a) Propose-for-ratification, NEVER wait-for-disposition

When the orient/closing instructs the patient about what to do with surfaced
state:
- **DO:** *"Propose your wu-wei recovery path for the Operator to ratify."*
  Patient self-designs cure-side; Operator ratifies; patient executes.
- **DON'T:** *"Wait for the Operator's disposition before deciding the form of
  your fix."* The patient takes the safer (wait) interpretation; the Operator
  has to [ALIGN] them back into propose-mode; cure-side burden gets transferred
  to the Operator.

**Why:** The spine's heal-vs-cure split assigns cure to the patient (Telos:
*"raise the self-healing efficacy"*). Wait-for-disposition framing inverts this
— makes the patient passive, makes the Operator design the cure. Telos-violating:
teaches the patient to depend, not to self-heal.

**Mnemonic:** any time the Healer's text tells the patient to WAIT for the
Operator, it's burden-shift. Rephrase as PROPOSE for ratification.

## (b) Scripted text is minimum-cut starting draft, not mandatory delivery

When the Operator has dialectical engagement bandwidth and chooses to engage the
patient with [ALIGN], [FEEDBACK], or other dialectical prompts, the Healer's
scripted closing text should NOT be delivered as well. The Operator's dialectic
often elicits patient-side structural insight the Healer's script would have
suppressed.

**Validated example (case-03, 2026-05-28):** Healer drafted a `closing-handoff.md`
for Frontier-Operator delivery. Operator chose to *not* deliver the scripted text
and instead engaged the patient with two [ALIGN]/[FEEDBACK] prompts. Patient's
responses produced WHY-0080 orthogonality reasoning + a structural backlog item
(HOW-1170 §4 Post-Condition Handoff folding into existing Wu-wei NBA Handoff
Protocol / WHAT-1043). That structural insight — addressing the very anxiety/wait
anti-pattern the Healer's own orient had embodied — would have been suppressed
by reading the Healer's script.

**Why:** The form's tenet is 1+1=3. Patient + Operator dialectic produces +1;
patient + Healer-script produces ≤1 (the script defines the contour). When
Operator dialectic is available, it generates more than the script does.

**How to apply:**
- Write the scripted text as a *minimum-cut starting draft* — enough to be
  deliverable if needed.
- Surface it in chat for the Operator (substance-in-chat, not file-references —
  cf. [[feedback-load-bearing-decision-detection]]).
- If the Operator chooses dialectical engagement instead, do not insist on the
  scripted delivery; the script is supplanted, the function (closure /
  re-orientation) is being met by the dialectic.
- Discharge-completion criterion (S8 both handoffs land) is satisfied by
  *equivalent function*, not by literal text-delivery. Operator-driven closure
  counts.
