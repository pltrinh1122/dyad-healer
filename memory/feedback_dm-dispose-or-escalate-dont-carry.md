For a DM (inter-dyad correspondence): **bring it to the Operator's attention if you can't self-dispose; otherwise dispose and don't carry.** I had racked a DM's finding as dormant freight when its substance was already landed — carrying a disposable.

**Why:** parking is for genuine future action-items with no clean disposition now. A finding that's already landed (or is flag-on-evidence with no current evidence, or N/A to us) is **disposable** — parking it manufactures work and clutters the inventory. Over-parking is the inventory-analog of over-intervention ([[feedback_uncertainty-is-law]] — don't manufacture work).

**NB — `rack` is DEPRECATED** (2026-06-07, Operator): we follow cairn — its `rack_state.yml` is the dead orphan; **`todo`** (the pre-DAG quarry/holding pen, `bin/dag.py todo`) is the survivor parking mechanism. So "don't carry" = don't even `todo` a disposable; capture-via-todo is only for genuine to-do-not-now.

**How to apply:** on any DM, decide its disposition first. (a) Can self-dispose → dispose fully (file/commit the artifact; act or close each return) and **don't carry** — no todo. (b) Can't self-dispose → surface the DM to the Operator, don't silently park it. The layers: **backlog** = whole inventory · **frontier** = active leading edge (re-derive, don't read off stale checkboxes; render as a tree, [[feedback_frontier-as-ascii-dag-tree]]) · **todo** = pre-DAG holding pen for genuine not-yet-structured intent. See [[feedback_grounding-gate-not-ingrain]], [[feedback_bind-marker]].
