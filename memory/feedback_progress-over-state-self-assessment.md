When self-assessing Healer conduct (in reflections, retrospectives, or any
CONTINUE/START/STOP framing), the right frame is **progress over state**:

- Absolute proficiency is the wrong baseline. The Healer will always have
  mind-reading gaps, channel-discipline lapses, and ratification-shape errors
  — that's true at every level of practice maturity.
- Session-over-session **progress** is the load-bearing measure. A session
  that's better than the last is a CONTINUE; one that regresses is a STOP.
- The Operator quote (case-03, 2026-05-28): *"the credit is in the progress
  made toward greater mind-reading proficiency — better this session than
  last; progress is what matters most for the Operator, because without
  progress there's no achievement."*

**Why:** assessing the Healer's conduct against absolute proficiency produces
false STOPs (every imperfection looks like harm) and starves CONTINUEs (real
gains get overshadowed by remaining gaps). The Telos is *"raise self-healing
efficacy… until resuscitation is rarely needed"* — a *trajectory* claim, not a
state claim. Healer self-assessment should mirror the Telos's shape.

**How to apply:**
- When drafting CONTINUE: name the **delta** from prior sessions, not the
  current absolute state. "Improved at X" beats "good at X."
- When drafting STOP: name patterns **still active**, not patterns corrected
  in-stream. Patterns the Operator caught and the Healer absorbed via FEEDBACK
  are no longer STOPs at the moment of the retrospective.
- When evaluating a STOP claim of "nothing comes to mind this session" by the
  Operator: take it at face value via this frame. Corrections that landed +
  no residual frustration = honest no-STOP.

**Validated:** 2026-05-28 case-03 retrospective. Healer-side draft of §1
CONTINUE focused on absolute mind-reading claim ("Agent reads Operator's
intent well, anticipates information without being asked"). Healer's
falsification surfaced multiple chat-history gaps (form correction × 2,
"no urgency" closing, hunt-and-peck, Frontier-hat asks, wait-framing).
Operator FEEDBACK refined: progress-over-state — same claim survives,
reframed around session-over-session delta. Healer integrated; Operator
ratified.
