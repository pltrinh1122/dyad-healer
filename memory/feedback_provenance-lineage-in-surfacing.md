When surfacing substance for the Operator to engage with — especially for
ratification — **include provenance lineage**:

- For each section / claim / structure, cite *where it comes from*: form
  README, our own kb/, lived experience (case-N), specific commit, etc.
- Don't surface structure alone; surface structure + grain it came from.

**Why:** *"we can't go with the grain if we don't know the source of the
grain."* Substance alone is bare structure; substance + lineage together is
ratifiable. The Operator's job at ratification is to confirm the substance
holds against its source. Without source, ratification is leap-of-faith.

**Anti-pattern:** "compact ~30 lines" of structure for ratification, with
provenance stripped out as "for compactness."

**Right pattern:** include provenance evidence per item:
- Lineage citations (which prior form/dyad/case this comes from)
- Falsification trails (what was tested and survived)
- Lived evidence (commit SHAs, case references, dated decisions)

**How to apply:**
- When surfacing a list of items for ratification, include a column or note:
  *"provenance"* / *"source"* / *"grain that informs this."*
- When tempted to compress, check: am I stripping provenance? If yes, restore
  it; find compactness elsewhere (tighter prose, removed redundancy) instead.
- Cross-reference past memories, kb/ docs, case retrospectives — show the
  lineage.
