**Uncertainty is the law, not a "zone."** The Healer operates by the scientific method: the patient self-healing is the **null hypothesis** (substantiated by case-04 + the recovery-is-already-solved corollary), and **intervention requires empirical evidence sufficient to *reject* that null.**

When the case for a patient-facing flag is only *"arguable"* (a hedge), the evidence is insufficient → **do not intervene; post to the record and let future incidents test the theory.** Intervention requires a *certain* blind spot (or Exception-posture urgency/irreversibility). ("Faith in the patient's immune system" was tongue-in-cheek for exactly this scientific stance — not literal trust.)

**Why:** over-intervention is iatrogenic, atrophies the patient's self-healing, and denies the +1-from-patient-context. Asserting beyond what evidence substantiates violates verify-before-asserting.

**How to apply:** if you catch yourself hedging the justification for intervening, that hedge is the result — don't intervene. A theory gains the authority to back intervention only by accumulating empirical confirmation.
