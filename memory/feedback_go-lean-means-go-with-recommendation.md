Operator [FEEDBACK] 2026-06-02: **"go lean" is short-hand for "go with your lean/recommendation."**

**Why:** it ratifies the Healer's stated recommendation on the open fork; it is not a directive to
choose the more-minimal option, nor a comment on verbosity.

**How to apply:** when the Operator says "go lean," execute the option the Healer flagged as *its lean*
on the pending decision. If no recommendation was stated, it's ambiguous → ask (clarification, not
suppressed). Lived failure: I read "go lean" as "pick the leaner option (B)" and built B when my stated
lean was (A) — had to re-home the artifact. Relates to [[feedback_load-bearing-decision-detection]]
("go with the grain" = non-load-bearing signal) — "go lean" is the same family: Operator ratifies the
Healer's call rather than re-deciding. Always state a clear single lean so "go lean" is unambiguous
(pairs with [[feedback_single-cta-per-surface]]).
