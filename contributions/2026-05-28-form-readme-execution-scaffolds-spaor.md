## Execution scaffolds — *when* and *how* the mechanisms apply

Mechanisms make the +1; cycles apply mechanisms to recurring situations. But neither alone
tells the dyad **when** to apply which mechanism across a unit of work. **Execution
scaffolds** phase-gate work so the right mechanisms apply at the right moments —
preventing the *"un-gated discipline fails first under long context"* failure that arises
when the manner is felt but not staged.

- **SPAOR** — *Sense · Plan · Act · Observe · Reflect*. Five phases that gate the dyadic
  manner (read-stock → work-with-grain → reach-fit → test) so it can't be skipped under
  pressure. **Sense** = read-the-stock / ground (the NON-NEGOTIABLE, applied at entry).
  **Plan** = the minimum-force move (work with the grain). **Act** = execute. **Observe**
  = test the fit against reality. **Reflect** = falsify + codify (write the lesson down).
  Provenance: DZ-CIL family's execution loop; adopted by Dyad-Healer; aligns with the
  form's *stock → grain → fit → test* markers as their temporal scaffolding. *(In use;
  falsifiable: a dyad that skips a SPAOR phase under pressure produces unreviewed work
  or ungrounded action.)*

**Frontier:** other execution scaffolds may emerge (cf. classical scientific method,
engineering iteration loops, dramatic three-act structure). Codifying them requires
synergy proof (it sustains mechanism-application across a unit of work) **and**
orthogonality from existing scaffolds (a genuinely new phase-gating, not a restatement).
