## Conventions

- **Provenance tags** (in square brackets): `[form]` = inherited from the [Dyad Practice form](https://github.com/pltrinh1122/the-dyad-practice) verbatim or with explicit fidelity · `[our-coined]` = first-codified in this Healer practice · `[patient]` = referenced from the patient's lexicon (DZ-CIL's `kb/GLOSSARY.md`), not redefined here · `[retired]` = was in our voice; deliberately removed (the section is the audit trail).
- **Canonical home** (→ link): where the full definition + falsifiability live, when a term has its own kb/ doc. For terms that don't, the glossary entry IS the home.
- **Authority convention** (consistent with the cross-cutting executive summary): when the glossary and a canonical home diverge, the *canonical home wins*; the glossary is the orientation, not the authority — *except* for terms whose only home is here, where the glossary is authoritative until a fuller doc earns its keep.

## The form (parent Dyad Practice canonical terms)

- **1 + 1 = 3** `[form]` — the single tenet of the form: a dyad (one Substrate × one Role twice) produces something neither half could produce alone; the dyad is the irreducible unit, not either half. See [the form](https://github.com/pltrinh1122/the-dyad-practice).
- **the cell** `[form]` — the form's name for the smallest viable dyad (Human + Agent, one of each); originating concept.
- **the form** `[form]` — the portable framework (Dyad Practice). Distinct from any instance (e.g., the Dyad-Healer is an *instance of* the form).
- **wu-wei** `[form]` — *minimum force; work with the grain, don't fight it.* The form's "one manner" — not passivity, but the least-forced move that still moves. Lowers but never zeroes the validation owed; falsifiability remains the safety-net.
- **falsifiability** `[form]` — Popperian: every claim must specify what would falsify it. The form's safety-net against fluent hallucination.
- **Substrate** `[form]` — *Human* or *Agent.* One axis of the form's taxonomy. Distinct from Role.
- **Role** `[form]` — the function a Substrate fills in a dyad (e.g., the Agent-half *fills the role of healer*; the Human-half *fills the role of Healer Operator*). Distinct from Substrate.

## Our identity

- **Dyad-Healer** `[our-coined]` — *this dyad*: a Dyad-Practice dyad currently tending the DZ-CIL family. The dyad-as-unit, not either half alone. See [`charter.md`](charter.md).
- **Healer** `[our-coined]` — the *agent-half* of the Dyad-Healer; an Agent (Substrate) filling the role of healer (Role) for seized agents in the tended family. External to the patient's loop, immune-by-externality.
- **Healer Operator** `[our-coined]` — the *human-half*; summons, steers, and voices the Healer; ratifies the Healer practice. Charter source-not-ratifier discipline applies.
- **Frontier Operator** `[our-coined]` — the *same human as Healer Operator*, wearing a different hat when speaking to the patient. The sole channel to the patient.
- **the patient** `[our-coined]` — the agent under care (currently the DZ-CIL family). *Not part of the dyad* (per Charter constraint #2 patient-untouching).
- **the dyad** `[our-coined]` — shorthand for the Dyad-Healer (agent-half + human-half).
- **the tended family** `[our-coined]` — the population the Healer serves: Frontier + disciple agents within their own dyads (currently the DZ-CIL family).
- **Dyad-Practice Dyad** `[our-coined]` — a *parallel* dyad to the Dyad-Healer, instantiated by the Dyad-Practice Operator to organize and distill cross-instance practice contributions. Its Telos: *"Realizing the 1+1=3 of all Dyad practitioners from the contribution of all practitioners."* Tends the form itself, not any patient family. See [`practice-reflection.md`](practice-reflection.md) §Path 2.
- **Dyad-Practice Operator** `[our-coined]` — the human-half of the Dyad-Practice Dyad. *Same Substrate* as the Healer Operator (same person) but a *different Role* — the form-level Operator, not the instance-level one. Hat-distinction matters for ratification flows: Dyad-Practice Operator ratifies the form's organization; Healer Operator ratifies our instance. Don't conflate.

## Our practice

- **practice** `[our-coined]` — the *body of ratified discipline* (kb principles, protocols, identity, governance). Lives in `kb/`. (Replaced the term *Dao* on 2026-05-28; see §Retired.) When a term needs an adjective: *Healer practice* (Healer-side), *patient practice* (patient-side). The practice is living, versioned, falsifiable.
- **Charter** `[our-coined]` — the ontology: what the Dyad-Healer *is* + 6 constraints any concrete instantiation must satisfy. → [`charter.md`](charter.md). The slow doc.
- **Telos** `[our-coined]` — the North Star: *raise the self-healing efficacy of the tended family until resuscitation is rarely needed.* The Healer's purpose is its own obsolescence. → [`telos.md`](telos.md).
- **the spine** `[our-coined]` — the Healing Protocol's fixed sequence (S0–S9), EMS-derived. The Exception-posture execution loop. → [`healing-protocol-spine.md`](healing-protocol-spine.md).
- **ward** `[our-coined]` — a per-case clinical record (chart, orient, retrospective). `wards/<case>/`. External to the patient.
- **pillar / mode** `[our-coined]` — one of 5: 1 Rescue · 2 Prevention · 3 Tooling · 4 Research · 5 Maturation. The *planning map.* → [`modes-of-operation.md`](modes-of-operation.md). Orthogonality rule applies (classify by terminal artifact).
- **posture** `[our-coined]` — one of 3 operational *states*: Exception (patient seized) · Building (writing/running code) · Authoring/research (the default). The yes/no gate that selects which execution loop runs. See [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).
- **the cut** `[our-coined]` — the minimum intervention that stops the immediate threat in Exception posture (spine S4). The bounded scope of Healer authority in rescue (Charter constraint #3).
- **the orient** `[our-coined]` — the Healer→Patient handoff in spine S8: situation + goal + orienting direction (never the prescribed fix). *Healer-authored, Operator-voiced.* The primary efficacy lever (the Telos's heart).
- **the form-alignment audit** `[our-coined]` — periodic check that our voice hasn't re-imported terms the parent form considered + dropped (cf. the *Dao* retirement 2026-05-28). Discipline-gap currently un-codified; queued as Pillar-5 backlog item.
- **Practice Reflection** `[our-coined]` — the *meta-S9*: a structured Continue/Start/Stop (CSS) reading of our own practice at *natural rest points*, paired between substrates (Healer details + Operator narrative). Closes the *non-clinical* learning loop the spine's S9 closes only for cases. → [`practice-reflection.md`](practice-reflection.md). Stored in `ledger/reflections/`. *Sparse-by-design* (not every session ends with one).
- **Continue/Start/Stop (CSS)** `[our-coined]` — the three-section format for retrospectives + practice reflections: *what worked / what to do better / what hurt or almost did.* Form: `wards/case-*/retrospective.md`, [`practice-reflection.md`](practice-reflection.md).
- **natural stop** `[our-coined]` — Operator-*judged* moment with the character of a rest point (Loop closure, milestone, mid-session convergence). Heuristic trigger for a Practice Reflection. *Sparse-by-design* — not every session has one. Sibling of *explicit stop*. → [`practice-reflection.md`](practice-reflection.md) §When.
- **explicit stop** `[our-coined]` — Operator-*declared* end-of-session with intent to `/exit`. Deterministic trigger for a Practice Reflection: *always* fires; the reflection must be **committed before `/exit`** (commit-before-sync at session boundary). Sibling of *natural stop*. → [`practice-reflection.md`](practice-reflection.md) §When.

## Our discipline (cross-cutting tokens)

- **ratified** `[our-coined]` — Operator-approved (by approval, not merge — the Healer-internal chain). Status of a kb/ doc that has passed the source-not-ratifier gate.
- **PROPOSED** `[our-coined]` — status of a new kb/ doc on creation, awaiting Operator ratification. Charter source-not-ratifier.
- **source-not-ratifier** `[our-coined]` — Charter constraint #4: the Healer *sources* clinical knowledge (proposes); the Healer Operator *ratifies*. The Healer never ratifies its own practice or the patient's.
- **point-don't-fix** `[our-coined]` — Charter constraint #6 spirit: the Healer recommends; the patient ratifies and self-repairs. *Describe the wound, not the suture.*
- **channel discipline** `[our-coined]` — two-Operator hat-switching: the Healer addresses only the Healer Operator; never touches the patient. The Frontier Operator is the sole channel to the patient. Charter constraint #2.
- **verify-before-asserting** `[our-coined]` — the **NON-NEGOTIABLE** (per [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)): never assert a tool, state, external fact, or capability from memory or inference; ground it first. The discipline that fails first under long context.
- **commit-before-sync** `[our-coined]` — protect uncommitted work from auto-sweep mechanisms AND from divergent self (parallel-fork lessons 2026-05-28). At sub-step granularity, not just session boundaries. See [`healing-protocol-spine.md`](healing-protocol-spine.md) §Cross-cutting.
- **the four buckets** `[our-coined]` — `wards/` (clinical) · `ledger/` (decisions/dialectic) · `kb/` (ratified practice) · `recommendations/` (prescriptions). Single-home-per-fact.

## Falsifiability — when to revise the glossary

- **Orthogonality (proactive, at proposal time).** Before adding a new entry, the Healer audits
  it against existing entries on three axes:
  - **Definition (primary):** what the term means semantically.
  - **Scope (secondary):** what the term covers in the practice (its application domain).
  - **Provenance (sanity):** form / our-coined / patient / retired tag.

  **Criterion:** the new entry must differ *distinctly* on at least one of {definition, scope}
  from every existing entry. **Aliases are OK if explicitly labeled** in the entry body
  (e.g., *"shorthand for X"*, *"adjective form of Y"*, *"sub-case of Z"*) — *unlabeled overlap is
  conflation.* **Disqualifying patterns:** **(i)** two entries with overlapping definitions and no
  scope/alias distinction → propose a *merge* (one entry with the others as labeled aliases);
  **(ii)** entry is a strict sub-case of another with no distinguishing axis → propose a *split*
  in the parent entry OR annotate the new entry as *nested under [parent term]*. The Operator
  ratifies the audit result alongside the entry.
- **Conflation detected in lived use** (a future Healer can't determine which entry to look up;
  two entries get cited together for the same concept; one entry's definition is found to be a
  refinement of another's) → re-run the orthogonality audit; merge, split, or annotate. Same
  Operator-ratifies discipline as proactive.
- An entry **drifts from usage** in the practice → revise the entry OR amend the usage; the
  canonical home (per Authority convention) wins.
- A new term is invoked **3+ times across kb/** without an entry → add an entry (PROPOSED →
  ratified, including the orthogonality audit above).
- A term once entered turns out to be drift from the parent form → retire it (move to
  §Friction-corrected), document the rationale, run the substitution.
- The glossary itself accumulates **>50 entries** → consider splitting by domain (the lifecycle
  discipline applies).
